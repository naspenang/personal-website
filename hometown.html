Welcome to Butterworth, Penang

I grew up in Butterworth, the principal town of Seberang Perai in the state of Penang, Malaysia.

Butterworth is well-known for its delicious local food, rich history, and the iconic Penang Ferry service that connects the mainland to Penang Island.


Scenic View of Butterworth

Things to do in Butterworth

  • Visit the Butterworth Art Walk
  • Enjoy the local Nasi Kandar and seafood
  • Take a ride on the Penang Ferry
